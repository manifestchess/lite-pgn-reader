its own licence and its own attribution requirement. If you copy anything out of
this directory, carry the credit with it.

The authoritative list, including the board images and everything else the app
ships, is in [COPYING.md](../../COPYING.md) at the repository root, a copy of
which ships inside the app under licenses/. This file is a convenience copy
for the piece sets, because this directory is the part people most often lift

## Before adding a set

Manifest Chess Lite is sold, so a NonCommercial licence (CC BY-NC-SA and friends) is
not usable here no matter how good the art is. "Freeware" is a price, not a
licence, and grants nothing. A set with no stated licence is all rights
reserved.
